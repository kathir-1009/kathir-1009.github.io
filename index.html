01 — Introduction

About me

I'm a web developer from Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, working at ShoutOut LLC since August 2025. I came into development through an Electronics and Communication Engineering degree, and that background still shows up in how I work — I care about how systems behave underneath the surface, not just how the screen looks.

Day to day I build and maintain responsive web applications with HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript, write reusable components, and follow the SDLC properly — version control, review, testing, and documentation. I enjoy the unglamorous parts: hunting down why something breaks at 320px, and making a component reusable enough that the next person doesn't have to reinvent it.

Alongside front-end work I train deep learning models. My final-year project is a NASNet-based CNN for multi-class skin cancer classification, and I've built small Android apps, worked with SQL and Power BI, and picked up Python for web development.
